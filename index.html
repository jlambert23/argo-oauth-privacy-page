Lambert Hermes App

Lambert Hermes App is a private personal assistant run by Justin Lambert for his own household. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

When someone signs in with Google, the app can access only the Google account data and services that person chooses to grant. That access is used only to help that person with their own information, at their request.

  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is processed and stored on a privately owned home computer, and is used only to provide the assistant's features.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
