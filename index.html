Lambert Hermes App

Lambert Hermes App is a private family assistant used only by Justin and Rachel Lambert. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

The app can access Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive for the account that signs in to it. That access is used only to help that person with their own email, schedule, and files, at their request.

  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is processed and stored on our own home computer, and is used only to provide the assistant's features.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
